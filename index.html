Our Vision & Inception

From Neglected Fruit Peel to Functional Culinary Science

Initiated under the HETI Student Youngpreneurship 2025 program by Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS), EKSOICE addresses severe agro-food waste by engineering healthy everyday indulgences for everyone.

Circular Bioeconomy

Collaborating with local farmers in Kampung Nanas Ngancar, Kediri to convert bulky pineapple peels that typically end up in landfills into valuable raw bio-materials.

Nutritional Wellness

Formulated specifically to offer a low-sugar, antioxidant-packed alternative for sweet-toothed kids and diabetic consumers seeking guilt-free indulgence.

Scientific Validation

Rigorously lab-tested at ITS for enzyme activity, total phenolic content, moisture, and sugar ratios to guarantee verified nutritional labeling.

Trilemma To Triumph

Bridging Agriculture, Waste, and Child Health

The Urgent Challenges

  • 1

    Severe Organic Agro-Waste

    According to agricultural statistics (BPS & Idelia, 2022), approximately 30% to 40% of every harvested pineapple fruit consists of peels that are instantly discarded without valorization.

  • 2

    Low Economic Farm Gate Margins

    Pineapple farmers face razor-thin profits selling whole fresh fruit, lacking access to downstream biotechnology that converts by-products into high-value commodities.

  • 3

    Childhood Sugar Overconsumption

    Mass-market confectioneries and industrial ice creams are overloaded with refined sugars, artificial coloring, and empty calories that spike obesity and weaken immune defense.

Status Quo: Linear Disposable Food System

The EKSOICE Solution

  • ✓

    Bioactive Enzyme Extraction

    We salvage pineapple peels through a proprietary brine-neutralization wash, extracting potent Bromelain enzymes, dietary fiber, and polyphenolic antioxidants.

  • ✓

    Economic Uplift for Local Growers

    By purchasing peels directly from vendors and cooperatives in Ngancar, Kediri, we create an auxiliary revenue stream, fostering rural regenerative circularity.

  • ✓

    Immune-Protective Everyday Treat

    Children receive an irresistibly creamy, naturally flavored ice cream that actively fortifies their immunity, digestion, and vitality—priced affordably at just Rp 5,000.

Result: Regenerative Circular Bio-Economy
Product & Chemical Engineering

Inside the EKSOICE Prototype

Developed under rigorous chemical engineering standards at ITS Vocational Faculty to preserve thermo-sensitive bioactive agents.

Step 01

Brine Decontamination

Washed in optimized saline brine solution to eliminate dirt, pesticide residues, and acrid sap compounds.

Step 02

Controlled Drying

Low-temperature dehydration preserves delicate enzymes and prevents thermal degradation of antioxidants.

Step 03

Compound Extraction

Solid-liquid extraction optimized for maximum yield of bromelain protease and polyphenolic flavonoids.

Step 04

Homogenized Churning

Formulated with premium dairy and natural sweeteners, producing silky overrun and consistent mouthfeel.
